### 1. Verdict

**Stop it.** Confidence: **75%**.

### 2. Why

1. **It inverted Robert's purpose.** *Read:* [map.md:51](map.md:51) seeks an ambitious program in Mo to drive what Mo gains next. By 2026-11-16, Robert put tables in the language, planning in the compiler, and storage in Mo's C runtime—dropping the 90-line Mo policy split even after Experiment 16 met every test for it ([design.md:345-350](design.md:345), [experiments.md:1077](experiments.md:1077)). *Inference:* MoDB is no longer a Mo program testing Mo. It is a 15-item rewrite of Mo ([design.md:362-379](design.md:362)) with no application pushing on it.
2. **Removing constraints broke selection.** *Read:* Robert ruled that today's Mo, build cost, and ease of learning are not limits ([experiments.md:11-12](experiments.md:11)), and closed [12-what-real-data-goes-in-first.md](issues/12-what-real-data-goes-in-first.md) and [14-what-rule-keeps-the-database-from-bending-mo.md](issues/14-what-rule-keeps-the-database-from-bending-mo.md) unanswered. *Inference:* That measure is wrong. AI makes writing C cheap, not proving it right; Mo's Lean proofs (machine-checked math on core types) omit C, crashes, and disk layout ([experiments.md:863-864](experiments.md:863)). Without real data or a cost cap, every hard problem gets pushed into the runtime, straining one person's review attention.
3. **Unanchored choices breed fake puzzles.** *Read:* Decision 3 keeps all arguments and replies forever to replay across upgrades ([experiments.md:1193-1195](experiments.md:1193)). In Experiment 18, dropping 10 of 30 calls changed 15 of the other 20 replies ([experiments.md:1387](experiments.md:1387))—an alternate history with no plan to apply it ([design.md:207](design.md:207)). That permanent log then forced Decision 4 ([experiments.md:1458](experiments.md:1458)): external vaults to erase one person's data. *Inference:* Speculative features are creating follow-up crises before storing one row.
4. **MoDB's own research undercut a new agent database.** *Read:* [overview.md:47-55](wiki/overview.md:47) found agent failures came from broad permissions and prompt injection (hostile instructions in data), not concurrent writes, and agents mostly use private databases. Language-locked formats killed object databases ([design.md:120](design.md:120)), and SQLite beats a new engine for ordinary apps ([design.md:122](design.md:122)). *Inference:* Per-caller tokens and narrowed read views solve the real risk on existing Mo services.
5. **It collides with Mo's live work.** *Read:* Mo has one real service ([experiments.md:925](experiments.md:925)), deployed 2026-11-16 for a 14-day Phase 6 live test with 4 of 8 access checks unrun ([live-service.md:14-16](~/code/mo/docs/ops/live-service.md:14), [live-service.md:136-146](~/code/mo/docs/ops/live-service.md:136)). Its 38,800-line C runtime may port to Rust in Phase 7 ([24-how-does-mos-runtime-become-memory-safe.md:14](issues/24-how-does-mos-runtime-become-memory-safe.md:14), [24-how-does-mos-runtime-become-memory-safe.md:41](issues/24-how-does-mos-runtime-become-memory-safe.md:41)). *Inference:* Writing C storage internals now grows the port burden mid-Phase 6.

### 3. What Had Value and What Did Not

*Facts read in files; value inferred.*

- **Had value:** [11-how-fast-and-large-is-a-mo-service.md](issues/11-how-fast-and-large-is-a-mo-service.md) and Experiment 19 ([experiments.md:1263](experiments.md:1263)) measured the 125,000-row ceiling, 58-hour replay cost, and 10 ms HTTP header tax. Experiment 5 ([sketches/instruction-counts/main.mo](sketches/instruction-counts/main.mo)) proved C built-ins scan whole lists for 26 steps, bypassing `--max-work` (Mo's per-call step cap). [in-place.mo](sketches/kept-versions/in-place.mo) caught Mo copying any `Map` inside a `struct`. Experiment 4 ([experiments.md:715](experiments.md:715)) showed agents follow a one-page query rule (40/40). Experiment 8 ([planner.py](sketches/planner/planner.py)) planned single-table reads from Mo Core (the compiler's small typed form) and caught arrival-order bugs. Experiment 16 ([policy.mo](sketches/store-policy/policy.mo)) and Experiment 17 ([Sweep.tla](models/Sweep.tla), a TLA+ tool checking every reachable protocol state) proved a 90-line Mo policy works and caught a crash bug on unpinned snapshot roots. [overview.md](wiki/overview.md) and [families.md](wiki/comparisons/families.md) pair every database strength with its cost.
- **Did not:** Experiments 9–14 ([experiments.md:332-655](experiments.md:332)) spent six rounds keeping join answers current on a toy shop with no store underneath. [Replay.tla](models/Replay.tla) and [Worlds.tla](models/Worlds.tla) checked 4–29 states where 88/88 runs matched hand predictions ([models/README.md:11](models/README.md:11), [models/README.md:48](models/README.md:48)). Decisions 2 (`R`), 3 (`D`), and 4 dropped the Mo split, kept old code runnable forever despite layout-free state export, and opened privacy-vault panels with zero data.

### 4. Strongest Case Against Stopping

Mo targets living services run by agents ([README.md:3-6](~/code/mo/README.md:3)), yet every service stops near 9 MiB, blocks reads behind writes, shares one token, and wipes its log every 100 calls ([design.md:366-372](design.md:366)). Fixing those piecemeal in Phase 7 risks bolting clumsy storage onto Mo. Designing them whole—types as schema, compiler-planned indexes, commit-checked promises, and snapshots sharing unchanged tree nodes—gives Mo a built-in data layer no SQL wrapper matches ([map.md:87](map.md:87)). Four days proved the compiler pass across 23,877 checks ([design.md:243](design.md:243)), and stopping at Decision 4 violates "doing it right outweighs doing it soon" ([map.md:51](map.md:51)).

### 5. Cheapest One-Week Test

Put MoDB's issues and decisions (~1 MiB, inside Mo's ceiling; [15-which-problems-is-this-database-for.md:36](issues/15-which-problems-is-this-database-for.md:36)) into a Mo service extending [asked-again.mo](sketches/kept-versions/asked-again.mo): per-agent snapshots ("worlds"), re-checking recorded reads on commit, and per-agent tokens. Touch zero C code. Have 2–3 agents work through it for five days.
- **Yes:** Worlds catch a real meaning clash, needless retries stay under 20%, and Robert keeps it on day 6.
- **No:** Agents rarely collide, recorded reads reject harmless edits ([models/README.md:27](models/README.md:27)), or Robert returns to files.

### 6. If He Stops

- **Carry into Mo ([ROADMAP.md](~/code/mo/docs/ROADMAP.md)):** Fix `--max-work` on C built-ins, `Map`-in-`struct` copying, double HTTP header parsing, and step-limit refusals stopping services. In Phase 7, add per-caller tokens, commit-time promise checks, concurrent read snapshots, and disk-backed collections using [policy.mo](sketches/store-policy/policy.mo)'s split and [Sweep.tla](models/Sweep.tla)'s pinned recovery root. Keep the [wiki](wiki/overview.md) and prediction-first rule ([experiments.md:9-10](experiments.md:9)).
- **Leave:** Compiler-kept join answers, eternal cross-upgrade call replay, `Held(T)` vaults, sensitivity types, and speculative worlds.
