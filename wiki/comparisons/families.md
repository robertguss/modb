# The ten families compared

One table of the ten families against the same columns, then the ideas a database for many AI agents could borrow, the ideas that rule each other out, the gaps no family fills, and the limits of the evidence. It is compiled from the wiki's family and idea pages, states what they say, marks inference, and recommends nothing.

## The ten families

*Overlapping work* means work by several clients whose spans in time intersect.

| Family | What it is for | Biggest strength | What pays for it | Biggest weakness | Overlapping work | What an agent struggles with |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Relational and transactional](../families/relational-and-transactional.md) | General transactional work for many users; programs that survive storage changes ([01](../../research/01-relational-and-transactional.md)) | Full isolation, and queries that say what, not how | Blocking, aborts or clock waits; an optimiser ([01](../../research/01-relational-and-transactional.md)) | Isolation-level names promise more than the engine gives ([01](../../research/01-relational-and-transactional.md)) | [Locks](../ideas/locks-held-until-commit.md) or [snapshots](../ideas/multi-version-snapshots.md); serializable engines abort and leave the retry to the caller | A transaction held open while it thinks stalls cleanup or locks; replaying the whole transaction after an abort ([01](../../research/01-relational-and-transactional.md)) |
| [Analytical and lakehouse](../families/analytical-and-lakehouse.md) | Few large reads over many rows ([02](../../research/02-analytical-and-specialised.md)) | Fast scans and high compression | [Columns](../ideas/column-storage.md) and [write-once pieces](../ideas/immutable-pieces-with-background-merge.md): slow single-row changes | Data visible before it is settled; slow small and concurrent writes ([02](../../research/02-analytical-and-specialised.md)) | Writers to one table block each other (Snowflake) or commit optimistically and retry (Iceberg) | Unsettled writes, lock timeouts that look like hangs, scan cost unseen in advance, cleanup nobody owns ([02](../../research/02-analytical-and-specialised.md)) |
| [Specialised stores](../families/specialised-stores.md) | One kind of question: time, words, meaning or place ([02](../../research/02-analytical-and-specialised.md)) | Speed for that question | A weaker or later answer: refresh, approximation, bounding boxes, in-order arrival | The weak answer is silent ([02](../../research/02-analytical-and-specialised.md)) | Writes invisible until a refresh; segments merged later | A search right after a write finds nothing; a filtered vector search returns too few rows with no error; backfills are costly ([02](../../research/02-analytical-and-specialised.md)) |
| [Key-value, document and distributed](../families/key-value-document-and-distributed.md) | Speed, scale or availability ([03](../../research/03-key-value-document-distributed.md)) | Writes accepted at scale, even in a network split | Transactions, schemas and joins given up; some came back narrowed ([03](../../research/03-key-value-document-distributed.md)) | The guarantee hides in settings, and acknowledged writes have been lost ([03](../../research/03-key-value-document-distributed.md)) | [Last writer wins](../ideas/last-writer-wins.md), [conflicts kept as versions](../ideas/conflicts-kept-as-data.md), or a [version check](../ideas/optimistic-concurrency.md) | Cannot see what guarantee it got; hidden conflict branches ([03](../../research/03-key-value-document-distributed.md)) |
| [Models that lost or stayed niche](../families/models-that-lost-or-stayed-niche.md) | One shape of data: trees, graphs, objects, triples, rules ([04](../../research/04-models-that-lost-or-stayed-niche.md)) | Fits that shape, such as long graph traversals | Navigation by hand, or speed that must beat relational alone ([04](../../research/04-models-that-lost-or-stayed-niche.md)) | Programs break when storage is reorganised ([04](../../research/04-models-that-lost-or-stayed-niche.md)) | Varies: optimistic commits (GemStone), read committed with lost updates (Neo4j), one serial writer (Datomic) | Clause order as query plan; 3.3% correct SPARQL without examples; schema-last leaves nothing to look up ([04](../../research/04-models-that-lost-or-stayed-niche.md)) |
| [Databases that keep history](../families/databases-that-keep-history.md) | Audit and "as of" queries ([05](../../research/05-databases-that-keep-history.md)) | Free history, and lock-free reads of any past moment | [One writer](../ideas/one-writer-at-a-time.md), storage, and a dearer "now" ([05](../../research/05-databases-that-keep-history.md)) | [Forgetting](../ideas/deleting-personal-data-from-history.md) is second-class ([05](../../research/05-databases-that-keep-history.md)) | One serial writer; compare-and-swap, `ASSERT`, expected versions | Forgetting "as of when?"; projections that lag; no interactive writes in XTDB ([05](../../research/05-databases-that-keep-history.md)) |
| [Versioned data and branching](../families/versioned-data-and-branching.md) | Reproducibility, review, safe experiments ([06](../../research/06-divergent-copies-and-merging.md)) | A [branch](../ideas/copy-on-write-branches.md) that is nearly free to make and discard | Shared storage: deep branches read slowly, or branching is slow (BranchBench) | Merges are structural; the products agents use most do not merge ([06](../../research/06-divergent-copies-and-merging.md)) | Each writer on its own branch; overlap seen only at merge | Choosing base, ours or theirs without either side's intent; clean merges wrong in meaning ([06](../../research/06-divergent-copies-and-merging.md)) |
| [Replication and sync](../families/replication-and-sync.md) | Offline copies, collaboration, fast local reads ([06](../../research/06-divergent-copies-and-merging.md)) | Every write accepted locally, with no locks across copies | Some rules cannot be kept at write time ([06](../../research/06-divergent-copies-and-merging.md)) | Silent winners; a rejected write surfaces late, away from its code | Pick a winner silently, stop and ask, re-run against an authority, or never merge | Building on a value that silently replaced someone's work; a [self-merging type](../ideas/data-types-that-merge-themselves.md) cannot stop two agents taking the last item ([06](../../research/06-divergent-copies-and-merging.md)) |
| [Code inside the database](../families/code-inside-the-database.md) | Removing the app tier; logic atomic with data; results kept fresh ([07](../../research/07-code-inside-the-database.md)) | The function is the whole transaction; the database retries it and drives subscriptions | [Determinism](../ideas/deterministic-functions-inside-transactions.md) (no I/O inside) and known reads and writes ([07](../../research/07-code-inside-the-database.md)) | The code is opaque to the database; one object is the throughput ceiling, with no transaction across objects ([07](../../research/07-code-inside-the-database.md)) | Optimistic with automatic rerun (Convex), one thread per actor, or locks and restart (Mnesia) | Hidden rules (no `fetch`, a 1-second budget), wide reads that conflict with every write, trigger cascades, double sends on retry ([07](../../research/07-code-inside-the-database.md)) |
| [Correctness-first](../families/correctness-first.md) | Correctness under crashes, lost messages and bad disks ([08](../../research/08-correctness-first-databases.md)) | Any failure replays exactly from one seed | Control of time, randomness, I/O and scheduling: no threads, few dependencies ([08](../../research/08-correctness-first-databases.md)) | Misses performance, liveness and workload blind spots; tests the vendor's code, not the user's ([08](../../research/08-correctness-first-databases.md)) | FoundationDB: optimistic, 5-second limit; TigerBeetle: one thread; SQLite: one writer | FoundationDB's 5-second limit, TigerBeetle's fixed schema, SQLite's busy errors ([08](../../research/08-correctness-first-databases.md)) |

What the table shows: each family dropped one assumption and pays for it somewhere ([00](../../research/00-landscape-overview.md)), and no family's answer to overlapping work suits an agent that reads, thinks for a long time, then writes. Locks and snapshots punish the long transaction, optimistic schemes abort it more the longer it runs, and branches and copies defer the clash to a merge that sees only what was written (inference from the rows above).

## Ideas worth borrowing

Ordered by how many of the nine gaps in the [overview](../overview.md) each idea helps close. The ordering, and which gaps each idea helps, is my inference from the idea pages; ties are broken by how many families the idea appears in. The gaps are numbered as in [What no family does](#what-no-family-does). Gap pages with no working system are listed there, not here.

| Idea | From | What it gives | What it costs | Gaps it helps |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Reads and writes known up front](../ideas/read-and-write-sets-known-up-front.md) | Key-value (DynamoDB), history (XTDB), code inside (Convex, Calvin) ([03](../../research/03-key-value-document-distributed.md), [07](../../research/07-code-inside-the-database.md)) | Conflict checks without locks, exact subscriptions, scheduling before running; no transaction held open | No back-and-forth inside a transaction; data-dependent sets need a guess and restart; compiler-derived sets are conservative | 7, 1, 4, 9 |
| [Labels that follow data](../ideas/information-flow-labels.md) | Research PostgreSQL (IFDB); Snowflake tag propagation in part ([20](../../research/20-keeping-sensitive-data-from-agents.md), [21](../../research/21-security-of-a-database-agents-use.md)) | Sensitivity, writer and trust carried into everything derived, checked on every exit | Label rules for every operator; side channels designed out; implicit flows; about 1% throughput in IFDB | 8, 9, 5, 2 |
| [Deterministic functions as transactions](../ideas/deterministic-functions-inside-transactions.md) | Code inside (Convex, SpacetimeDB, Prevayler), history (Datomic), sync (Zero) ([07](../../research/07-code-inside-the-database.md)) | Retries, replay and subscriptions inside the database | No I/O inside; enforcement or discipline; time budgets; wide reads conflict | 7, 6, 1 |
| [Writes that carry their assumptions](../ideas/writes-that-carry-their-assumptions.md) | Sync (Bayou, Zero), history (Datomic `:db/cas`, XTDB `ASSERT`, KurrentDB) ([06](../../research/06-divergent-copies-and-merging.md), [05](../../research/05-databases-that-keep-history.md)) | A write rejected or reworked when what its writer believed no longer holds | A guard per write, an ordering authority, tentative reads (Bayou); writers must state assumptions | 1, 3, 2 |
| [One writer at a time](../ideas/one-writer-at-a-time.md) | Six families: SQLite, DuckDB, Redis, Datomic, Prevayler, TigerBeetle ([00](../../research/00-landscape-overview.md), [05](../../research/05-databases-that-keep-history.md), [08](../../research/08-correctness-first-databases.md)) | Serializability without locks; a schedule a simulator can own; checks run inside the writer | One writer's throughput; writers wait; long transactions block everyone | 6, 3 |
| [Rules the database enforces](../ideas/invariants-the-database-enforces.md) | History (Datomic, XTDB), versioned (Dolt), correctness (TigerBeetle), niche (TypeDB), document (MongoDB) ([05](../../research/05-databases-that-keep-history.md), [06](../../research/06-divergent-copies-and-merging.md)) | Bad writes refused; rules checked over a merged result | Schema first; coordination for rules a merge cannot keep; one domain (TigerBeetle) | 3, 1 |
| [Conflicts kept as data](../ideas/conflicts-kept-as-data.md) | Key-value (Dynamo, CouchDB), sync (Automerge, Notes), versioned (Dolt) ([03](../../research/03-key-value-document-distributed.md), [06](../../research/06-divergent-copies-and-merging.md)) | Nothing lost; resolution an explicit step that can block commit | Every reader handles versions; someone must resolve, without intent | 1, 4 |
| [Cheap branches that share storage](../ideas/copy-on-write-branches.md) | Versioned (Neon, Dolt, lakeFS, Chronos) ([06](../../research/06-divergent-copies-and-merging.md), [09](../../research/09-agents-and-databases-today.md)) | Try a change on a copy, keep or discard it | Slow deep reads or slow branching; no way back without a merge | 1, 4 |
| [Three-way merge](../ideas/three-way-merge-of-data.md) | Versioned (Dolt, lakeFS) ([06](../../research/06-divergent-copies-and-merging.md)) | Branch work brought back without a person when sides touched different things | Stable keys; "no conflict" only means no shared cell | 1, 4 |
| [Per-statement check in the engine](../ideas/statement-authoriser.md) | Relational (SQLite authoriser) ([21](../../research/21-security-of-a-database-agents-use.md)) | Checks the parsed statement, not its text | Fewer allowed statement forms; a grant language small enough to read | 5, 9 |
| [Short-lived, narrowed grants](../ideas/attenuated-capabilities.md) | Security practice (Biscuit tokens), not a family ([21](../../research/21-security-of-a-database-agents-use.md)) | Each task's credential cut to its rows and rights, expiring soon | Issuing and renewing; offline tokens hard to revoke; enforcement where the data is | 5, 9 |
| [Planning before reading untrusted data](../ideas/plan-then-execute-agents.md) | Agent harness research (CaMeL, FIDES), not a family ([21](../../research/21-security-of-a-database-agents-use.md)) | The only proven defence found: untrusted data cannot choose actions | 77% of tasks solved against 84% undefended; a custom interpreter; implicit flows | 9, 7 |
| [History the agent cannot erase](../ideas/history-and-audit-the-agent-cannot-erase.md) | History (QLDB, Oracle Flashback) ([21](../../research/21-security-of-a-database-agents-use.md)) | Undo after a destructive change; traceable theft | Storage, a second authority; the log is itself personal data | 2 |
| [Facts added, never changed](../ideas/append-only-facts.md) | History (Datomic, KurrentDB, Kafka) ([05](../../research/05-databases-that-keep-history.md)) | History, time travel and audit for free | Storage grows; deleting is second-class; one writer | 2 |
| [Storing events instead of state](../ideas/storing-events-instead-of-state.md) | History (KurrentDB, Kafka), code inside (Prevayler) ([05](../../research/05-databases-that-keep-history.md)) | A causal record many readers can replay | A separate read model that lags; one writer per stream | 2 |
| [Serializable by default](../ideas/serializable-by-default.md) | Relational (CockroachDB, Spanner) ([01](../../research/01-relational-and-transactional.md)) | No write skew breaking a rule | Aborts and retries, clock waits, or one writer | 3 |
| [Optimistic concurrency](../ideas/optimistic-concurrency.md) | Most families ([03](../../research/03-key-value-document-distributed.md)) | Lost updates turned into visible conflicts, without blocking | Retries; aborts grow with transaction length | 1 |
| [Multi-version snapshots](../ideas/multi-version-snapshots.md) | Relational (PostgreSQL, Oracle, InnoDB) ([01](../../research/01-relational-and-transactional.md)) | Readers never wait for writers | Old versions kept until the oldest reader ends; write skew | 1 |
| [Data types that merge themselves](../ideas/data-types-that-merge-themselves.md) | Sync (Automerge, Yjs), niche (GemStone) ([06](../../research/06-divergent-copies-and-merging.md)) | Concurrent edits combined with no one asked | Cannot reject a write; arbitrary same-field winner | 1 |
| [Deterministic simulation testing](../ideas/deterministic-simulation-testing.md) | Correctness (FoundationDB, TigerBeetle) ([08](../../research/08-correctness-first-databases.md)) | Rare fault chains found and replayed | Control of time, randomness, I/O, scheduling; misses performance | 6 |
| [Schema and examples served to agents](../ideas/schema-and-examples-for-agents.md) | Niche (triple stores), versioned (Neon MCP) ([04](../../research/04-models-that-lost-or-stayed-niche.md)) | Correct SPARQL from 3.3% to 75.75% in one benchmark | A declared schema; examples kept correct | 5 |
| [Row and column security](../ideas/row-and-column-security.md) | Relational (PostgreSQL) ([20](../../research/20-keeping-sensitive-data-from-agents.md)) | Whole rows or columns withheld | Constraint checks bypass it; the agent cannot read the policy | 5 |
| [Two timelines](../ideas/valid-time-and-system-time.md) | History (XTDB, SQL:2011) ([05](../../research/05-databases-that-keep-history.md)) | Corrections keep both belief and fact | Every query needs "as of when?"; period-splitting writes collide | none |
| [Time-travel queries](../ideas/time-travel-queries.md) | History, analytical ([05](../../research/05-databases-that-keep-history.md)) | Read the data as it was | Storage; someone must own expiry | none |
| [Grow-only schema](../ideas/grow-only-schema.md) | History (Datomic), code inside (SpacetimeDB) ([05](../../research/05-databases-that-keep-history.md)) | History never rewritten | Breaking changes refused; old formats converted on every read | none |
| [Staged schema changes](../ideas/staged-schema-changes.md) | Relational (Spanner F1, CockroachDB) ([01](../../research/01-relational-and-transactional.md)) | Schema changes without locks | No longer one atomic step | none |

## Ideas that cannot be combined

From each idea's "Cannot be combined with" section and the family tradeoffs.

| Taking this | Rules out or weakens | Why |
|---|---|---|
| [Self-merging types](../ideas/data-types-that-merge-themselves.md), [last writer wins](../ideas/last-writer-wins.md) | [Rules the database enforces](../ideas/invariants-the-database-enforces.md) that need a write refused: uniqueness of a chosen value, auto-increment ids, "never below zero" | A merge that never rejects cannot keep a rule unless any two valid states always merge into a valid one ([I-confluence](https://www.vldb.org/pvldb/vol8/p185-bailis.pdf); [06](../../research/06-divergent-copies-and-merging.md)) |
| [Last writer wins](../ideas/last-writer-wins.md) as the final answer | [Conflicts kept as data](../ideas/conflicts-kept-as-data.md) | One drops the loser, the other keeps it; CouchDB and Automerge pick a winner for plain reads and keep losers aside ([06](../../research/06-divergent-copies-and-merging.md)) |
| [Reads and writes known up front](../ideas/read-and-write-sets-known-up-front.md), fully serial writes ([serializable by default](../ideas/serializable-by-default.md) in XTDB) | Interactive transactions: read, decide, write inside one open transaction | The whole request must exist before it runs ([03](../../research/03-key-value-document-distributed.md), [05](../../research/05-databases-that-keep-history.md)) |
| [Locks](../ideas/locks-held-until-commit.md), [snapshots](../ideas/multi-version-snapshots.md), [optimistic concurrency](../ideas/optimistic-concurrency.md), [time limits](../ideas/transaction-time-limits.md) | An agent holding a transaction open while it calls a model | Locks block others; the oldest snapshot stalls cleanup; aborts grow with length; a limit aborts it ([01](../../research/01-relational-and-transactional.md), [08](../../research/08-correctness-first-databases.md)) |
| [Deterministic functions](../ideas/deterministic-functions-inside-transactions.md), [optimistic](../ideas/optimistic-concurrency.md) or deadlock retries | Side effects such as HTTP inside the transaction | A rerun repeats them: Mnesia sends twice; SpacetimeDB procedures may run several times on different states ([07](../../research/07-code-inside-the-database.md)) |
| [Deterministic simulation](../ideas/deterministic-simulation-testing.md), [one writer](../ideas/one-writer-at-a-time.md) | Operating-system threads, dependencies doing their own I/O, hidden nondeterminism; throughput beyond one writer | The test must own time, randomness, I/O and scheduling ([08](../../research/08-correctness-first-databases.md)) |
| [Facts added, never changed](../ideas/append-only-facts.md), [history the agent cannot erase](../ideas/history-and-audit-the-agent-cannot-erase.md) | [Deleting personal data](../ideas/deleting-personal-data-from-history.md) | An immutable record cannot hold personal data in any form, clear, encrypted or hashed, per EDPB reasoning (inference); the audit log is itself personal data ([05](../../research/05-databases-that-keep-history.md), [21](../../research/21-security-of-a-database-agents-use.md)) |
| Fast [branching](../ideas/copy-on-write-branches.md) | Fast reads on deep branches | BranchBench: 5 to 4000 times slower reads, or 25 to 1500 times slower branching ([06](../../research/06-divergent-copies-and-merging.md)) |
| [Masking by role](../ideas/dynamic-masking.md), [row security](../ideas/row-and-column-security.md) | A reader that writes its own queries, which is what an agent is | Filters run on the real value; constraint checks leak hidden rows ([20](../../research/20-keeping-sensitive-data-from-agents.md)) |
| [Consistent pseudonyms](../ideas/consistent-pseudonyms.md) | Unlinkable records, and tasks that need the real entity | What makes joins work makes linking work; retrieval fell up to 47 points ([20](../../research/20-keeping-sensitive-data-from-agents.md)) |
| [Planning first](../ideas/plan-then-execute-agents.md), [session narrowing](../ideas/session-tainting-after-untrusted-reads.md) | Flexible agents on mixed tasks | Mixed tasks need a fixed plan or a person; CaMeL lost about 7 points of task success ([21](../../research/21-security-of-a-database-agents-use.md)) |
| [Serializable](../ideas/serializable-by-default.md) with aborts | Clients with no retry path | Every write needs one; CockroachDB offers read committed for this reason ([01](../../research/01-relational-and-transactional.md)) |
| [Staged schema changes](../ideas/staged-schema-changes.md) | One atomic schema change inside a larger transaction | Staging trades atomicity for no locks ([01](../../research/01-relational-and-transactional.md)) |
| [Database inside the process](../ideas/database-inside-the-application-process.md) | Many processes writing one database | LevelDB allows one process; DuckDB one writing process ([02](../../research/02-analytical-and-specialised.md), [03](../../research/03-key-value-document-distributed.md)) |
| [Columns](../ideas/column-storage.md), [write-once pieces](../ideas/immutable-pieces-with-background-merge.md), [object storage](../ideas/object-storage-as-source-of-truth.md) | Cheap single-row changes; reads settled when the write returns; one layout for transactions and analytics | Every system pays with rewrites, a merge window, or a second copy ([02](../../research/02-analytical-and-specialised.md)) |

Three tensions follow from these rows (inference). The ideas that best serve the gaps, known reads and writes, deterministic functions and one writer, all forbid the interactive, side-effecting transaction an agent would naturally hold open. Never-refusing merges, which suit branches and offline copies, cannot keep the rules that gap 3 asks for. And keeping everything conflicts with forgetting a person.

## What this comparison cannot tell you

- **Whether agents hold transactions open.** It decides which overlap answer fits. Research 01 found no study and reasoned; 08 and 09 cite one 2026 workshop paper ([transaction time limits](../ideas/transaction-time-limits.md)). Research 09 judges at about 65% confidence that optimistic concurrency and branches already cover minutes-long overlap.
- **How much any defence reduces harm.** Research 21 found no measurement for least privilege, undo or approval ([attenuated capabilities](../ideas/attenuated-capabilities.md), [writes held for approval](../ideas/writes-held-for-approval.md)). The security rows above rest on incident reading, not trials.
- **How often agents hit each gap.** My ordering counts gaps equally. Nothing in the wiki measures how often agents meet a meaning-level merge conflict, a missing reason or a tainted session, and the Berkeley paper's 20× branches and 50× rollbacks are Neon data, attributed differently by research 06 and 09 ([branches that reconcile](../ideas/branches-that-reconcile-with-each-other.md)).
- **Speed.** Research 09 cites the Berkeley paper expecting agent query volume to be the bottleneck, against coordination first ([overview](../overview.md)). No page measures what "fast enough that an agent never waits" needs, and Mo's speed is unmeasured ([map](../../map.md)).
- **Old or vendor figures.** The 3.3% SPARQL result used 2024 models ([schema and examples](../ideas/schema-and-examples-for-agents.md)). Neon's 80% and 97% agent figures, Dolt's "conflicts very rarely", TigerBeetle's 1,024 cores and turbopuffer's 10x are vendor claims; ScramDB, FlureeDB, Yugabyte Meko and SurrealDB Agent Memory are unverified launches.
- **Disputes that touch a row.** Whether Neon and Xata can merge ([copy-on-write branches](../ideas/copy-on-write-branches.md)); whether graph stores beat relational on traversals ([Neo4j](../systems/neo4j.md)); whether pseudonymised extracts stay personal data after *EDPS v SRB* ([legal de-identification](../ideas/legal-de-identification.md)); what TEE.fail broke ([confidential computing](../ideas/confidential-computing.md)).
- **Thin sources.** Most agent re-identification papers are 2026 preprints ([how hidden data is recovered](../ideas/re-identification-by-linking.md)). Xata, Turso, Supabase, FIDES, ScramDB, Yjs, consensus and MCP have no page, and about 160 paragraphs lack an inline citation ([log](../log.md)).
- **Combinations nobody built.** The incompatibility table lists only clashes a page names. Ideas never tried together, such as labels with three-way merge, may clash in ways the wiki cannot show (inference).
- **Ideas outside the families.** The privacy and security ideas mostly live beside databases or in agent harnesses, so the family table says little about them.
